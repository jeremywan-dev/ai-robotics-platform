# Project Requirements (V1)

## Overview

The goal of V1 is to build a reliable, modular, and cost-effective four-wheel mobile robot platform with basic remote control capability. The platform should provide a solid foundation for future expansion, including autonomous navigation, Raspberry Pi integration, and AI applications.

## Scope

V1 focuses on building a reliable hardware platform with basic remote control capability. Advanced functions such as autonomous navigation, computer vision, and AI will be implemented in future versions.
---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1
The robot shall use a four-wheel drive chassis.

### FR-2
The robot shall be able to move forward, backward, turn left, turn right, and stop.

### FR-3
The robot shall be able to receive external movement commands.

### FR-4
The robot shall be capable of measuring its own motion and orientation.

---

## Power Requirements

### PR-1
The robot shall be powered by a rechargeable battery.

### PR-2
The robot shall support USB-C charging without removing the battery from the chassis.

### PR-3
The robot shall include a power switch for turning the system on and off.

### PR-4
The robot shall provide a battery level indicator to display the remaining battery capacity.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-1
The system shall be reliable.

### NFR-2
The system shall be modular and easy to extend.

### NFR-3
The system shall be designed with cost-effective hardware while maintaining reliability and expandability.
